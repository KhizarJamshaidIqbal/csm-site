Questions, answered

Understand the product before you build.

Is CSM another AI website builder?

No. CSM is the commerce backend. You choose your frontend design and development tools, then integrate your storefront with CSM.

How do MCP and the storefront API differ?

MCP gives authorized AI agents access to store operations. The public REST API is the storefront-facing interface. An administrative MCP token must never be included in browser code.

Does every AI builder connect automatically?

No. The workflow depends on the client's MCP support, your configuration, and the integration code. CSM does not claim official partnerships with the builders named here.

Will I still need setup and testing?

Yes. Hosting, permissions, provider credentials, integration, and end-to-end testing still matter. CSM is intended to reduce repeated backend work, not guarantee error-free operation.

Is the walkthrough a live product demonstration?

No. It is an illustrative browser simulation. Contact the team to discuss a product demonstration and verify capabilities relevant to your use case.

What does joining early access mean?
