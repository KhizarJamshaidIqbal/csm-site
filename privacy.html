Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Who We Are

CSM Engine (“we”, “us”) operates this website to provide information about our autonomous e-commerce backend platform and to collect early access and investor inquiries. This policy explains what personal information we collect and how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily submit through our forms:

  • Early Access Waitlist: your name, work email, company/venture fund, applicant role, and preferred frontend builder.
  • Pitch Deck Request: your fund or angel name and investor email address.

We do not collect payment information, precise location data, or any special category data. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics on this site.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To reserve your place on the early access waitlist and contact you about onboarding.
  • To send qualified investor requests the confidential pitch summary and data room link.
  • To send occasional product and funding updates related to CSM Engine. You can opt out at any time by replying to any email.

4. Processors We Use

Form submissions are delivered through Web3Forms (a form-to-email delivery service), which processes your submission solely to deliver it to our inbox. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Local Storage

To keep a backup of your submission on your own device, we store a copy of the details you entered in your browser’s local storage. This data never leaves your device except through the form delivery described above, and you can clear it at any time via your browser settings.

6. Security & Compliance

Submissions are transmitted over HTTPS. We apply the principle of data minimization: we ask only for the fields needed to respond to your request, and we retain them only as long as needed for that purpose or as required by law. No system is perfectly secure; please do not send confidential information through these forms beyond what is requested.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU/EEA under GDPR and California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port the personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email founders@csmengine.dev.
