Company & Team

Building the Autonomous Operating System for Commerce.

CSM Engine is developed by EPSOL Dev, an engineering firm specializing in headless WordPress infrastructure, Model Context Protocol servers, and enterprise automation.

The Thesis

Generative UI tools (Lovable, v0, Bolt, Cursor) collapsed the cost of building a storefront interface to nearly zero. But a storefront without a transactional backend cannot take a single order: payments, taxes, inventory atomicity, returns, fraud resistance, and customer service remain months of specialized engineering.

That separation — generative front-end, mission-critical back-end — was inevitable. What was missing was a standard interface the two could speak. The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is that standard. CSM Engine bets that MCP becomes the universal contract between AI agents and commerce infrastructure, and we are building the deepest, most battle-tested commerce tool catalog on that protocol: 317 production tools spanning payments, inventory, returns, flash sales, SEO, and conversational CRM.

The result: a prompt becomes a transacting store in minutes, with 0% platform take-rate and full data sovereignty — the "Stripe + AWS" layer for AI storefronts.

The Origin & Team

CSM Engine was architected by founder Khizar Jamshaid Iqbal and the engineering team at EPSOL Dev. The company operates across dual hubs: registered corporate headquarters in London (128 City Road, London EC1V 2NX) and core software engineering operations in Sialkot.

Prior to creating CSM Engine, the team engineered high-traffic custom WooCommerce and WordPress architectures, including sister platform Custom Blog Management (CBM). Recognizing the structural limitations of WordPress post meta for high-frequency stores, the team extracted that machinery into 36 custom relational database tables and wrapped them in an introspectable Model Context Protocol server.

Reference Customer: Lux Wearz

CSM Engine serves as the core commerce back-office for Lux Wearz, an apparel brand operating on a decoupled architecture. The storefront uses CSM's REST API for catalog browsing and cart checkout, while autonomous agents leverage CSM's MCP tools for real-time inventory reconciliation, returns processing, and conversational care over WhatsApp.

Engineering Principles

  • Additive-first (Rule 8): Agents must plan safe, additive mutations before touching critical transactional paths.
  • 0% Platform take-rate: We monetize hosted compute, streaming capacity, and enterprise gateways — never merchant transaction volume.
  • Sovereignty by default: Merchants own their database, customer records, and inventory history on their own server infrastructure.
  • Permission safety: Three-tier token security (read-only, approval-required with single-use tokens, and full) prevents LLM hallucination risk.
