Catalog & Transactional Core (Products 8 · Orders 11 · Inventory 6)

Inventory locking, dynamic variant matrices, cart CSRF tokenization, and multi-currency exchange.

`products_set_variants` Generates complex multi-attribute matrices (Size, Color, Material, SKU, Barcode) in a single autonomous step.
`inventory_adjust` Concurrency-safe atomic stock decrementing during checkout drops to eliminate double-selling.
`orders_timeline` Granular state machine tracing order lifecycles from pending ➔ payment_captured ➔ packing ➔ fulfilled.
Enforces strict X-CSM-Cart-CSRF tokens on every checkout to prevent cart forgery exploits.

Payment & Checkout Engine (Payments 3 · Security 2 · Cart CSRF)

Dual credential Live/Sandbox storage, zero-leak secret masking, and automated HMAC roundtrip verification.

`payment_methods_save` Saves Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, Bank Wire, and COD settings with automated test token verification.
`payment_gateway_test` Simulates live checkout authorization and webhook roundtrips without touching merchant bank accounts.
Dual-Credential Vault Toggle between Sandbox and Live without erasing previously saved API keys or webhook secrets.
Zero platform transaction fee. 100% of payment gross margin stays with the merchant.

Post-Purchase & RMA Suite (Returns 22 · Care 13)

Self-service returns, automated inspection logs, restocking formulas, and gateway refund dispatch.

`returns_inspect` Evaluates returned physical items against warehouse condition criteria and records defects.
`returns_refund_preview` Calculates proportional tax and shipping refunds with optional restocking fee deductions.
`returns_settle` Dispatches automated refund via original Stripe charge ID and logs complete audit trail.
Streamlines post-purchase RMA workflows through structured autonomous decisioning and approval tokens.

Growth, Flash Drops & Marketing (Flash 8 · Coupons 5 · SEO 5)

Cycle timers, countdown status bars, coupons, personalized customer care cards, and subscriber alerts.

`flash_sale_set` Instantly schedules dynamic time-gated discounts with synchronized frontend countdown banners.
`flash_reminders_save` Collects high-intent customer emails and triggers pre-drop notification broadcasts automatically.
`coupons_create` Creates single-use, tiered, or cart-minimum discount coupons with granular usage tracking.

Conversational AI & Chat Assistant (Chat 23 · WhatsApp 4)

Multi-instance WhatsApp bridge, 1000-question adversarial golden-set QA test harness, and gap detection.

`whatsapp_agent_connect` Pairs your store with official WhatsApp Business API for interactive rich order cards and tracking updates.

`care_card_send` Dispatches visual status cards with interactive call-to-action buttons directly into customer chat threads.
